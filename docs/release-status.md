# Penny release status — 12 November 2026

Penny has a signed Android App Bundle, a saved Play Store listing and an internal testing draft. It is **not ready for a public rollout**: live authentication, billing, reviewer access, required Play declarations and the closed-test gate still need completion.

## Verified in this workspace

- 62 unit/database tests and 22 browser flows pass on 12 November 2026. The browser suite covered first-run setup, expenses and income, wallets, budgets, imports and exports, notifications UI, large numbers at 320 px, and the Premium sign-in gate. The production web build and Android sync pass. The browser suite now starts its own local server; it does not depend on the preview server.
- The latest bundle is at android/app/build/outputs/bundle/release/app-release.aab (built 12 November 2026). The signature checker reports “jar verified.” Google Play accepted it as version 1 (1.0), target SDK 36, in an **internal testing draft** named “Penny 1.0 internal test.” The upload key and signing settings are ignored by Git; back them up securely before publishing any release.
- The Play Console app exists as app.penny.expenses. Its English listing contains the description, Penny icon, feature graphic and four genuine app-screen captures. Google Play accepted the AI asset labels and saved the listing in Publishing overview. A local asset audit found those phone captures exceeded Google's 2:1 dimension limit, so five fresh 1080 × 1920 app captures and a 24-bit feature graphic were prepared in [release-assets](../release-assets/README.md) on 12 November. The new files are **not yet uploaded** because Play Console browser access is denied. The app-level trader status and Penny Terms of Service URL are saved. No release has been published or sent for review.
- Four penny_ database migrations and four penny- Edge Functions are live in Supabase project vcfalijtdakuyyfbdghy, shared with another app. Penny tables use row-level security. Penny deletion removes Penny data while leaving the shared sign-in identity and other-app data intact.
- Published [privacy](https://sites.google.com/view/penny-rzstudios/privacy-policy), [terms](https://sites.google.com/view/penny-rzstudios/terms-conditions), and [deletion](https://sites.google.com/view/penny-rzstudios/account-deletion) pages are linked in Penny and Play Console where applicable. The public site homepage says “Coming soon to Google Play.”
- Supabase accepts an email OTP request. Inbox delivery, code verification, Android callback and Google sign-in have not been proven end to end. Penny's native sign-in passes app.penny.expenses://auth/callback explicitly; the Supabase redirect allow-list includes it. The shared project's fallback Site URL is still localhost, so a live native sign-in test is required. A Penny Google Cloud project and Web OAuth client were created on 12 November; its only redirect URI is the Penny Supabase Auth callback. The client secret is not stored in this repository.

2. **Email and Google sign-in:** Rotate the Gmail app password shared during development, then enter the replacement directly in Supabase custom SMTP. Use neutral RZ Studios OTP templates because Auth email templates are shared by every app in this Supabase project. Test new email signup, returning-user OTP, Google sign-in, and both Android cold-start callbacks on the Play-installed build. Do not place client secrets in Git or public frontend configuration.
3. **Billing:** A Google Payments profile exists, but Play Console still reports that a merchant account is required before Subscriptions can open. Payout details were incomplete when last observed; the publisher must finish and verify the merchant setup, including the correct legal and bank country. Then create subscription `penny_premium` with a paid monthly US$5 target base plan and no trial, and configure an Android Publisher API service account with minimal rights. 4. **Console declarations:** Play Dashboard shows 8 of 12 app-information tasks complete. Finish Sign in details, content rating, target audience and Data safety. Target audience is blocked by incomplete Sign in details. Starting the content-rating questionnaire accepts IARC terms and requires the publisher's own decision. Check every declaration against the final build and published policy.
5. **Device validation:** Add authorized internal testers, publish the internal draft after sign-in and Premium access are ready, then install it from Play. Follow the [internal-to-closed-test checklist](internal-to-closed-test.md) for Android notifications, offline persistence, import/export with real files, sync opt-in and two-device conflicts, deletion, font scaling, accessibility and paid purchase states. 6. **Testing gate:** This Play Console explicitly requires a closed release with at least 12 opted-in testers for at least 14 days before applying for production. It currently shows 0 opted-in testers. Public deployment cannot occur before that period completes.
7. **Worldwide availability:** Review local privacy, consumer, subscription, tax and cross-border obligations for each offered region. The app, generated legal text and automated checks do not establish worldwide legal compliance. Limit launch countries if a requirement cannot be met.

Rotate the database and Gmail credentials exposed during development before launch. Keep the new values, upload key and service-account JSON out of this public repository and chat.
