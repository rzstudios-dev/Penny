# Penny

## A little more clarity for your money.

Penny makes everyday spending feel easier to understand. Give each expense a place in a cheerful envelope, set a budget, and see what you have spent and what is left at a glance.

### Simple from the first tap

Create a wallet in your own currency and start tracking right away. Add expenses or income in seconds, plan for recurring income, and explore clear reports when you want a closer look. Bring your existing records with a CSV or Excel import, and export a backup whenever you need one.

### Your money stays yours

Penny saves your records on your device by default. No sign-in is needed to get started. If you want a backup across devices, you can sign in and choose to turn on cloud sync. Penny has no ads and does not connect to your bank.

### Free to begin, room to grow

Start with one wallet, 10 envelopes and the budgeting essentials for free. Penny Premium adds unlimited wallets and envelopes, more playful icons and deeper reports.
