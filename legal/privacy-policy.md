# Penny Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 5 October 2026
**Publisher:** Khandoker Ashik Uz Zaman, Bangladesh
**Contact:** rzstudios.dev@gmail.com

Penny is a personal expense tracker. You can use its core features without signing in. Financial records stay on your device unless you choose to enable cloud sync. Penny does not connect to banks, access contacts or location, display advertising, or sell personal data.

## Information Penny handles

Penny stores the wallet and envelope names you create; income, expenses, amounts, dates, titles and notes; currencies, budgets, recurring-income schedules and app preferences. These records are saved locally on your device. Anyone who can open an unlocked device may be able to access them. Local records are not end-to-end encrypted, so protect your device and keep an export backup if the records matter to you.

If you sign in, Supabase Authentication processes your email address and an account identifier. Google sign-in may also provide a profile name. Signing in does not turn on cloud sync. If you enable cloud sync, Penny sends your ledger to our Supabase database so you can restore it on a signed-in device. Access rules limit each ledger to its account owner. Connections use HTTPS, and Supabase applies its storage and service security protections. Cloud sync is not end-to-end encrypted. Supabase may process technical logs needed to operate and secure the service.

For Premium, Google Play processes payment. Penny receives the product identifier, purchase token, subscription status and expiry information needed to verify access. We do not receive full payment-card details. If you redeem a Premium code, we store the membership status, activation time and a limited attempt counter to prevent repeated guessing. Google retains its own purchase records under its policies.

CSV or spreadsheet imports are read on your device. The original file is not uploaded. Imported entries become part of your ledger and are uploaded only if you enable cloud sync. Exports go only where you choose to save or share them.

If enabled, Penny schedules short budget and tip reminders on your device according to your selected frequency. A separate weekly reminder can invite signed-out users to sign in for optional backup. You can turn these off in the app or in Android settings. Reminder text does not include transaction amounts.

## Why we use this information

We use your records to show budgets, transactions, recurring income and reports; your sign-in information to authenticate you; optional cloud data to provide backup and sync; purchase data to verify Premium; and technical records to maintain security and reliability. We do not use financial records for advertising.

## Sharing and international processing

Financial records are shared with Supabase only when cloud sync is enabled. Supabase handles authentication when you sign in. Google handles Google sign-in and Google Play purchases when you use those services. Data may be processed outside Bangladesh or your country of residence by these providers. Their own privacy terms also apply. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect the service from abuse, subject to applicable law.

## Your choices, retention and deletion

You can use Penny locally without an online account, turn cloud sync and reminders on or off, edit or delete records, and export transactions or a full backup. Local data remains until you delete it, clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app. Cloud data and account information remain until you delete your account, subject to temporary provider backups and records that providers or law require them to keep.

In Penny, choose **Profile → Delete account & data** and type **DELETE** to remove your Penny account, cloud ledger, membership record and this device’s local ledger. Offline copies on other devices must be removed there. Deleting the Penny account does not cancel a Google Play subscription; cancel it in Google Play first. You may also request account and data deletion at **rzstudios.dev@gmail.com**. We will verify ownership before processing the request. See our separate account and data deletion page.

You can contact us at **rzstudios.dev@gmail.com** to request access, correction or deletion, or to ask a privacy question. Rights that apply under your local law remain available to you.

## Children and updates

Penny is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. Please do not enter another person’s sensitive information in a transaction note. If we materially change this policy, we will update this page and give appropriate notice in the app before the change takes effect.
