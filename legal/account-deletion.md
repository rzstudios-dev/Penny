# Delete your Penny account and data

**Publisher:** Khandoker Ashik Uz Zaman, Bangladesh
**Support:** rzstudios.dev@gmail.com

You can delete your Penny account and its associated data at any time, without a Premium subscription.

1. Open Penny and go to **Profile → Delete account & data**.
2. Read the warning, type **DELETE**, and confirm.
3. Penny removes the signed-in account and its cloud ledger, membership and code-redemption records, and this device’s saved ledger.

If you cannot open the app, email **rzstudios.dev@gmail.com** with the subject **Penny account deletion request** from the email associated with your Penny account. Do not send passwords, OTP codes or payment-card details. We will verify account ownership and process the request, or contact you if we need more information.

Export your records first if you want to keep a copy. Offline copies on other devices must be cleared on those devices. Temporary service backups and purchase records held by Google may remain under those providers’ retention policies or legal obligations.

**Important:** Deleting your Penny account or uninstalling the app does not cancel a Google Play subscription. To stop future charges, cancel it in **Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions** before deleting your Penny account.
